Feature 01
Campaign-driven recording
Vendors receive contextual prompts tied to ECCBC campaigns — in Darija, French, or Arabic. A built-in teleprompter guides delivery without scripting every word, preserving authentic voice while ensuring brand alignment.
multilingual · offline-aware
Feature 02
AI video assembly pipeline
Submitted clips are automatically assembled with Phase 1 B-roll, branded templates, and music. The vendor receives a finished, compliant video — no editing skills required. Moderation runs before assembly completes.
cloud render · brand-safe
Feature 03
Milestone & points engine
Streaks, regional leaderboards, and milestone badges sustain vendor engagement over time. Points translate to tangible ECCBC-defined incentives — driving network-scale behavior change that S1 and S2 cannot achieve.
gamification · retention
Feature 04
Personal performance view
Vendors see their own views, submission history, approval status, and regional rank — making impact tangible and motivating continued participation. Designed for low digital literacy with icon-first navigation.
analytics · simple UX
